## Disclosed issuer definitions

### Debt / Equity

The prospectus defines Debt/Equity as debt divided by total equity. Debt comprises long-term borrowings, short-term borrowings, and lease liability. It describes total equity as equity share capital plus reserves and surplus at the stated balance-sheet dates.

Source locator: prospectus page 11, definition section (NSE PDF lines 1230–1234).

Research implication: the disclosed debt perimeter includes lease liabilities. Do not silently substitute borrowings-only debt, and do not infer that this perimeter applies to another issuer.

### Return on Equity

The prospectus defines ROE as profit for the period divided by average equity, with average equity calculated as the average of opening and closing equity for the period.

Source locator: prospectus page 12, definition section (NSE PDF lines 1293–1295); repeated in KPI notes around prospectus page 177.

Research implication: this definition requires period-aligned profit and opening/closing equity on the same reporting basis. Do not substitute closing equity or owner-attributable equity without separate issuer evidence.

### Return on Capital Employed

The prospectus defines ROCE as EBIT divided by capital employed. It defines capital employed as tangible net worth plus debt plus deferred tax liability.

The prospectus contains a wording difference that must remain visible:
- The definition section says EBIT is profit for the period plus finance costs and total tax expense (page 12; lines 1293–1295).
- The detailed risk/KPI explanation says EBIT is profit for the period plus finance costs and total tax expense **excluding other income**; capital employed is tangible net worth plus debt plus deferred tax liability (page 67; lines 6415–6416; also repeated in the KPI notes around page 177).

Research implication: the exclusion/perimeter wording is not fully consistent across prospectus sections. This is not sufficient to activate a ROCE formula. Require current issuer filing evidence and reconciliation to reported statement facts; keep blocked until the precise EBIT bridge and capital-employed components are resolved.

### Interest Coverage Ratio

The prospectus defines Interest Coverage Ratio as EBIT divided by Finance Cost (KPI peer-comparison notes around prospectus page 177; NSE PDF lines 17255–17256).

Research implication: this is a historical issuer prospectus definition, not a universal interest-cover definition and not interchangeable with a separately labelled Interest Service Coverage Ratio. Require exact current filing, basis, period, EBIT evidence, and finance-cost evidence before considering a candidate.
