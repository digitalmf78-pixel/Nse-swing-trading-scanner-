- Lease liabilities are separately disclosed. They are excluded from the borrowing figures above and must not be added unless an explicit approved metric policy requires them.
- The figures are balance-sheet instant facts, not duration facts.
- The filing reports 30 September 2026 as unaudited and 31 March 2026 as audited. Preserve those statuses and the source URL with each fact.
