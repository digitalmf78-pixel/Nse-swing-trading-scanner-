# Revenue Growth — official NSE filing evidence

Research date: 2026-11-25

## Scope and safety boundary

This note records official NSE evidence for the identity and interpretation of the Revenue from Operations row, reporting basis, period, unit, and material comparability caveats. It is not a prior-year growth calculation, organic-growth assessment, universal QName approval, or production approval.

## Revenue statement-row evidence (INR lakhs)

The Financial Results — IndAS table reports:

| Row | Quarter ended 30-Sep-2026 | YTD ended 30-Sep-2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue from operations | 30,57,840.00 | 60,10,170.00 |
| Other income | 50,736.00 | 1,16,307.00 |
| Total income | 31,08,576.00 | 61,26,477.00 |

The filing's note says Revenue from Operations includes “Gross Revenue from sale of products and services” and “Other operating revenue”. This is a reported statement-row definition, not a universal rule that all issuers will use identical taxonomy concepts or extensions.

The financial-results table exposed in this filing gives the current quarter and current YTD columns; it does not provide a prior-year comparative value in those two columns. Therefore this filing alone cannot support a same-period YoY growth calculation. Do not manufacture or infer the prior-year denominator from another table or basis.

## Material comparability warnings explicitly disclosed by ITC

1. Century Pulp and Paper business was acquired effective 1 August 2026. The current quarter and YTD results include it from that date; the issuer explicitly says the results are not comparable with previous periods.
2. Sproutlife became a subsidiary effective 1 April 2026. The current quarter and six-month results include it; the issuer explicitly says those results are not comparable with previous periods.
3. The issuer says GST and GST Compensation Cess are excluded from gross revenue from sale of products, whereas Excise Duty is not excluded. Following expiry of GST Compensation Cess, GST and Central Excise duty on cigarettes increased effective 1 February 2026. The filing says reported gross revenue and Excise Duty are not strictly comparable with previous periods.
4. These disclosures show why same-calendar-period alignment and matching concepts alone do not certify organic or like-for-like revenue growth. Preserve the issuer's warnings; do not invent acquisition, excise, tax, or organic-growth adjustments.

## Separate segment schedule — do not silently substitute

The segment reporting schedule separately reports Revenue from Operations of INR 30,43,101.00 lakhs for the quarter and INR 59,84,083.00 lakhs YTD, after total segment revenue and inter-segment revenue. These amounts differ from the Financial Results table's Revenue from Operations values above.

Treat these as distinct source-table contexts. Do not substitute segment-schedule revenue for the Financial Results row, or reconcile the difference by assumption. Any future use must identify the exact table, row, context, and official statement reconciliation.

## Research-pipeline implications

1. Retain the exact current/prior fact gates: same issuer, same exact XBRL concept QName, same unit, same standalone/consolidated basis, undimensioned group context, and exactly aligned calendar periods.
2. Preserve raw filing values, period start/end, reporting type, basis, unit/currency, source URL, filing approval date, retrieval date, and original/revision resolution.
3. A current-period fact without a proven matching prior-year fact remains insufficient for growth. Missing prior data must stay missing; no zero or proxy denominator.
4. Store material acquisition, disposal, tax, excise, and accounting comparability disclosures as review flags. Do not label mechanical growth as organic growth.
5. This is one issuer's evidence. It does not prove a universal RevenueFromOperations QName or universal issuer presentation.
