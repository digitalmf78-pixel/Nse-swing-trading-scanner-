## Mechanical comparisons (not production metrics)

Using the official PDF comparative row:
- Q2 same-period comparison: (13,196.54 / 7,121.48 - 1) × 100 = 85.31%
- H1 same-period comparison: (24,838.89 / 12,708.60 - 1) × 100 = 95.45%

These are arithmetic reproductions of the filed row values. They are not an independently validated production metric. In particular, the prior-year comparison is available in the official PDF, while the NSE iXBRL Financial Results table exposes current-period columns only in this filing. A future automated XBRL pair must not invent or silently source a missing prior fact; it must preserve the PDF as a separately identified official evidence source and pass the established source/reconciliation gates.

## Comparability and context warnings

1. The issuer's note 4 says the quarter ended 30 September 2025 is the balancing figure between the limited-reviewed H1 FY2025–26 results and audited FY2024–25 results for the quarter ended 30 June 2025.
2. The issuer's note 5 says the quarter ended 30 September 2026 is the balancing figure between H1 FY2026–27 results and the Q1 FY2026–27 results.
3. The issuer states that production of copper rods for captive consumption and warehousing at its new in-house Upcast Facility commenced effective 4 August 2026. The current comparison therefore spans a change in operating setup; do not describe the mechanical growth as organic or like-for-like without further issuer evidence.
4. The company was listed on NSE and BSE on 23 December 2025 following its IPO. The historical Q2 comparative is a company result, but the issuer's note 4/5 balancing-figure methodology must be retained.
5. Revenue row values are not to be replaced by total income, segment values, sales volume, or investor-presentation rounded figures.
