## Attribution and interpretation controls

1. For consolidated Profit Growth candidate logic, use profit attributable to owners of parent, not unallocated total group profit. Keep non-controlling-interest profit as a separate raw fact.
2. The difference between owner-attributable profit growth and total-profit growth is visible in both periods. The two numerators must not be interchanged silently.
3. Revenue Growth uses Revenue from Operations, not Total Income. Other income remains separate.
4. Q2 is compared only with Q2; H1 YTD is compared only with H1 YTD. Do not compare a quarter with a half-year amount.
5. Both filings are consolidated, unaudited, in INR lakhs, and refer to the same issuer. Do not mix SJS standalone values with these consolidated comparisons.
6. These are mechanical reported growth comparisons, not organic growth. Acquisitions, disposals, consolidation perimeter changes and accounting changes may affect comparability.
7. The current and prior NSE integrated filing records have been located, but complete revision-history resolution and independent official-PDF reconciliation remain outstanding. Do not treat this note as satisfying those hard gates for a production candidate.
8. The Q2 FY27 filing has blank D/E, DSCR and ISCR fields; blanks are missing evidence, not zero and not permission to derive those ratios.
